1. PROJECT PURPOSE & CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MediKiosk is a kiosk-based patient history taking & clinical verification platform.
Before seeing a physician, patients answer an adaptive intake interview guided by clinical
decision trees and upload previous medical documents (prescriptions, lab reports). 
The backend automatically synthesizes both data sources into a structured clinical snapshot.

NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN RULE:
  "AI collects and organizes. Doctor verifies and decides.
   Every fact MUST carry an explicit source tag (🎤 interview / 📄 document)."

4. GROQ LLM SERVICE & PROMPTS (app/groq_service.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application implements 3 specialized Groq prompts:

1. Interview Question Generator Prompt (generate_interview_question):
   - Input: Chief complaint, target decision tree question, prior Q&A history.
   - Constraint: Must pick strictly from decision tree branches (Chest Pain, Fever, Cough,
     General); forbidden from inventing unapproved medical branches.
   - Function: Formulates empathic, natural phrasing tailored to patient context.

2. Document OCR & Extractor Prompt (extract_document_info):
   - Input: Medical report/prescription file details & base64/text.
   - Output: Structured JSON containing diagnoses, medications, lab values, dates,
     and an overall confidence score (0.0 - 1.0).

3. Record Synthesis & Flagging Engine (merge_record_and_flag):
   - Input: Interview responses JSON + Extracted documents JSON.
   - Function: 
     a. Tags every fact as "interview" (🎤) or "document" (📄).
     b. Detects Contradiction Flags (discrepancies between patient answers and docs).
     c. Detects Attention Flags (high-risk symptom combinations).
     d. Phrases all flags as "recommend prompt clinical assessment", NEVER as a diagnosis.
